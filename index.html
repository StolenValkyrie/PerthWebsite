A home for Australian Roblox roleplay

Perth Roleplay is a Discord community based in Perth, Western Australia, for players who want Australian-style roleplay on Roblox.

We’re gathering players ahead of an upcoming Australian roleplay game inspired by ERLC and ERAU. Our server is the place to meet people, find sessions, and be ready when the game arrives.

Our goals are simple: keep roleplay realistic, give creative players room to build characters and stories, and keep the community welcoming to newcomers and veterans alike.

We’re an independent community

Perth Roleplay is a fan-run Discord community. We do not own, develop or officially represent the Roblox game. All game content belongs to its respective creators, and any trademarks belong to their owners.

Community expectations

Everyone here is expected to:

Treat members and staff with respect, on and off the roleplay.

Take roleplay seriously and keep to realistic, fair scenarios.

Follow Discord’s Terms of Service and Roblox’s Community Standards.

Keep content safe and age-appropriate, with no harassment or discrimination.

Follow staff instructions and report problems through tickets.
